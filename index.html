Top universities in Malaysia and their specialties

马来西亚重点大学与优势专业

Malaysia has many universities with different strengths. Here are popular choices for international students.

马来西亚高校类型丰富、各有特色。以下是国际学生常见选择方向。

University of Malaya (UM)

Specialties: Medicine, Engineering, Computer Science, Social Sciences.

优势专业： 医学、工程、计算机、社会科学。

Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM)

Specialties: Agriculture, Food Science, Business, Environmental Studies.

优势专业： 农业、食品科学、商科、环境研究。

Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM)

Specialties: Pharmacy, Health Sciences, Education, Research programs.

优势专业： 药学、健康科学、教育学、科研方向。

Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM)

Specialties: Engineering, Natural Sciences, Arts, Sustainability studies.

优势专业： 工程、理科、人文艺术、可持续发展。

Taylor's University

Specialties: Hospitality, Business, Communication, Design.

优势专业： 酒店管理、商科、传媒、设计。

Monash University Malaysia

Specialties: Medicine, Psychology, Data Science, Engineering.

优势专业： 医学、心理学、数据科学、工程。
